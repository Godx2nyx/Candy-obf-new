Why CandyObf?

Every layer adds a new barrier against reverse engineering.

🖥️

Register-Based VM

Compiles your Lua to custom bytecode that runs inside a per-build virtual machine. Every build is different.

🔀

Opcode Permutation

Opcodes are randomly shuffled each build. Emulator signatures become useless instantly.

🔐

String Encryption

All string literals are XOR-encrypted with rolling keys and decoded lazily at runtime.

🎭

Variable Renaming

Local and global names are replaced with pseudo-random identifiers seeded per-build.

🛡️

Anti-Emulation

Fibonacci checks, math consistency tests, metatable probes, and coroutine traps crash emulators.

🍭

Roblox Ready

Output runs cleanly in Roblox Luau. No external dependencies, no require() chains.
